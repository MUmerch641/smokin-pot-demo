How the class runs

Every class follows the same rhythm — market first, then cooking, then a long lunch of everything you made.

1

Pick-up & market tour

Vannak picks you up free from your hotel in Battambang. Walk the local village market together and pick out fresh ingredients for your dishes.

2

Cook at the family home

Head to Vannak's family home in the countryside and cook your three chosen dishes from scratch — pounding curry paste, steaming amok, stir-frying lok lak.

3

Eat & take the recipes

Sit down to the meal you cooked, leave with Vannak's recipe book of Khmer and Thai dishes — and get dropped back at your hotel.

Good to know

Where do we meet?

You don't need to go anywhere — hotel pick-up in Battambang is included free. Vannak collects you, takes you to the market, then out to the family home for the cooking, and drops you back afterwards. Prefer to come yourself? The restaurant is on Street No. 1.5 in Battambang.

How do I pay?

The class is $15 per person, paid in cash on the day — US dollars or Cambodian riel are both fine. Nothing is charged when you book online.

Can vegetarians join?

Yes — every dish can be made vegetarian or vegan. Just pick the option when you book, and tell Vannak at the market if there's anything you don't eat.

How far ahead should I book?

A day or two ahead is ideal so Vannak can plan the market shopping, but same-day bookings are often possible for the afternoon class — send the booking and he'll confirm on WhatsApp.
